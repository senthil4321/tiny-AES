ISO 21434 Process Overview

1. Context & Scope

In this phase, the organization establishes the cybersecurity context, defines the boundaries of the system, and identifies the assets and interfaces that need protection. This step lays the foundation for the risk management process.

2. Risk Assessment (TARA)

A Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (TARA) is performed to identify potential threats, evaluate vulnerabilities, and assess the risk level of different scenarios. This helps prioritize mitigation measures based on severity and likelihood.

3. Security Concept

Based on the risk assessment, a comprehensive security concept is developed. This outlines the high-level security goals, design principles, and strategies to address the identified risks.

4. Technical Requirements

The security concept is translated into specific technical requirements. This phase defines the measures, controls, and system architectures necessary to achieve the cybersecurity objectives.

5. Implementation & Verification

Cybersecurity measures are implemented according to the defined requirements. Rigorous testing, validation, and verification ensure that these measures function as intended and mitigate the identified risks effectively.

6. Incident Response & Maintenance

The final phase focuses on preparing for and responding to cybersecurity incidents, as well as ensuring ongoing security through regular monitoring, updates, and continuous improvement of the cybersecurity posture.
